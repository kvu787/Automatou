This repository and project is named "Automatou", but that is just a general name to describe the
game mechanics.

Currently, there is only one narrative in Automatou: Bastion Absolute.

Bastion Absolute uses the archetype of technologically advanced humanity vs biologically advanced
aliens as a base. Example inspirations are Terran vs Zerg from Starcraft, humanity vs Tyranids from
Warhammer 40,000, Starship Troopers, Helldivers, and the Alien/Aliens franchise.

However, it diverges it several key ways:

- Humanity's advantage is technology *and* magic, instead of just technology.
  - to make for an even match-up against the aliens' biological advantage.
- The total number of units for evenly matched human vs alien forces are roughly even.
  - This means there is no massive swarm of zergling-like aliens against a much smaller force of humans.
  - This is partly motivated by the game's pixel art and square grid design:
    - A single square is the size of the smallest unit.
    - For humanity, the smallest unit is a dog/cat or similar pet-like creature.
    - A human occupies 4 squares
    - 

Bastion Absolute is the story of humanity's struggle against an existential alien threat.

A Bastion is a type of human soldier.
