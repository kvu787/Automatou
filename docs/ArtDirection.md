# Art direction

Automapolis uses original 16×16 tactical pixel art with the compact readability of classic console and handheld turn-based strategy games. The production assets are not generated by an image model: their authoritative source is the palette-indexed [`sprites.json`](../Shells/Godot/assets/sprites/sprites.json), compiled deterministically into PNG files.

## Visual grammar

- Square 16×16 canvas and nearest-neighbor display.
- Top-down/three-quarter tactical viewpoint with a fixed upper-left light.
- One shared palette: deep navy outlines, muted mineral surfaces, cyan aether, violet dream matter, and warm gold technology.
- Terrain fills its square. Beings, buildings, and effects use transparency and layer over terrain.
- Strong silhouettes take priority over internal detail.
- Selection, status, ownership, and targeting remain separate Shell overlays.
